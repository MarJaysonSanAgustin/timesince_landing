## Try one with your water filter

1. Tap **+** in TimeSince. In Explore, search for **Replace water filter** or tap **See all** beside **Around the house**.
2. Open the idea and tap **Try this**. You can rename it “Fridge filter” and set **Start date** to the day you fitted the current filter.
3. Review the two-month reminder. Change the interval to match your filter or switch it off. Tap **Save**. If the start ticket appears, **Slide to start**.
4. The saved counter now shows how long the filter has been in use. When you fit the next one, log the change. You can put the model number in **Notes** for your next shopping trip.

If you also keep a filter in a pitcher, you can [duplicate the counter with an empty history](/scenarios/too-many-near-identical-counters). For more ways to use those dates, read [Tracking irregular chores](/guides/tracking-irregular-chores).
