Everywhere else in this app, logging something resets its counter to zero: you did the thing, so the interval starts again. Quitting mode inverts that. The number climbs for as long as you stay away, and it only returns to zero when you say you slipped.

That is the one case where a climbing number is unambiguously the right shape.

## What a reset actually does

It starts the count again. That is all.

Nothing is deleted, nothing is marked failed, and the stretches you already counted stay in the calendar. If you went eleven days, then slipped, then went nineteen, the record holds both — and the second number is the one that came after you restarted, which is the thing most worth knowing about yourself.

This matters because the alternative is what [streaks do](/guides/why-streaks-backfire): return to zero and take the history with them, so that the price of one bad day is everything you had built. People do not usually lose one day to a broken streak. They lose the habit.

## Why this one is allowed to climb

Asking for a streak on a habit you are building [gets a deliberate no here](/scenarios/i-want-a-streak-counter). A quit counter is the exception, and the reason is that the thing being measured is genuinely cumulative.

Days since you last smoked is a real quantity. Days in a row that you remembered to stretch is a scoring system someone invented. The first describes the world; the second describes your compliance with a target. Only one of them is worth putting on a home screen.

## Read the calendar, not just the number

The headline number tells you about now. The calendar tells you about the pattern, and the pattern is usually the more useful of the two.

Four stretches of eleven days reads very differently from one unbroken eleven. Both show "11" today. If you only look at the number, you cannot tell which one you are living in.

## What this is not

It is a counter. It records what you tell it, and it shows you intervals.

It does not know what you are quitting. It cannot tell a slip from a pattern, it will not escalate, and it has no idea whether today's number is good news for you or not. If what you are quitting has physical withdrawal, or if it is affecting your health, work or relationships, this belongs alongside a clinician or a dedicated service rather than instead of one.
