A water filter, the car and a call home fit on Free. If you also want to track the cat’s flea treatment, you can archive a counter you no longer use to make room. Archived counters keep their history and sit outside the active-counter limit. With Pro, all four can stay active.

## What do you want to record?

If you need the last service date and a note about the repair, Free covers both. To plot odometer readings over time or keep a photo of each receipt, use Pro to add those fields. If your subscription ends, you can still fill in fields you already added.

For hands-free logging, Pro gives you [built-in Siri phrases and Shortcuts actions](/features/siri-shortcuts). Pairing an NFC tag also takes Pro. The [Home Screen widget](/features/home-screen-widget) is free.

## Timers and goals in version 1.16

With Pro, add a timer to an interval counter to record how long a session lasts. Timers on different counters can run at the same time, with controls on the Lock Screen. The ordinary time-since display is free.

Set one goal per counter with Pro. For an interval counter, that could be three walks in one week. For a quit counter, choose a length of time since your last reset. When you reach the goal, it goes into your history. You can choose another whenever you like.

For example, you can time a reading session and then log its duration. If you only want to remember which evening you last picked up the book, a free counter and a note about the chapter are enough.

## Which backup do you need?

[Private iCloud sync and dated backups](/features/icloud-backup) are free. Sync merges changes across your iPhones. Restoring a dated backup replaces this phone’s data with the saved version. Pro adds file export and import for people who want to keep a portable JSON copy.

## On the lifetime option

At US prices, three annual payments total $44.97. Lifetime access is $39.99 once. If your counters, notes and widgets already cover what you need, you can keep using Free.
