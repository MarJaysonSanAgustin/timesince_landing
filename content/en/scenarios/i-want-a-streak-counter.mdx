Someone asked for this on the feedback board, in as many words: seeing a current streak would be more motivating, and not wanting to break it is what keeps them going.

That is a real description of how streaks work for a lot of people, and it is not wrong.

## The reply was a no, and it explains itself

The developer's answer is the clearest statement of what this app is for:

> TimeSince intentionally avoids daily streaks for habits you're building. I want you to be able to come back after a day or a week without feeling like you've lost what you already did.

That is a product decision, not a missing feature. A streak is the one number in a habit app you can lose outright, and losing it tends to take the habit with it, which is the problem [streaks were supposed to solve](/guides/why-streaks-backfire).

## What you get instead

The counter is the headline: time since you last did it, climbing. It never resets itself.

Underneath that, Stats does the job a streak was doing badly. It calculates the average interval you actually keep for each counter and charts how that interval trends over weeks. If you water the plants every five days, it says five days. If that drifts to nine, you can see the drift, which is information rather than a penalty.

## If the streak was genuinely what helped

Then be honest that this app is arguing with you, and try Quitting mode for anything you are stopping rather than starting. The counter climbs for as long as you stay away, which is a chain in everything but name, except that a slip restarts the count instead of erasing it.

For habits you are building, there is no equivalent, and there is not going to be one.
