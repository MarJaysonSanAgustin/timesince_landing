The request put it plainly:

> It would be nice if we could have a single widget that displays multiple trackers/categories at once [...] That way, we wouldn't have to add a separate widget for every tracker and take up so much space on the Home Screen.

It shipped in version 1.9. The medium widget shows four counters, the large shows eight.

## Why one-per-widget stopped working

A widget per counter is fine at two. At five it is a home screen page, and the thing widgets were supposed to buy you — a number you can read without opening anything — gets slower than opening the app, because now you are scanning a grid of near-identical tiles for the one you wanted.

Grouping fixes the scanning problem as much as the space one. Four counters in a known order are read as a block.

## Choosing the four

The constraint is the useful part. Almost everyone keeps more counters than they check, and being forced to name four tends to reveal which ones were actually load-bearing.

A good test: which counters would you be annoyed to find out of date? Those go in the widget. The rest are fine living in the app, where you will see them when you open it.

## What still shows one

The small widget and the lock screen widget each carry a single counter. That is a size constraint rather than a limitation — a number small enough to fit eight into a lock screen widget would not be readable at a glance, which defeats the purpose.

If a particular counter needs to be unmissable, [a lock screen widget showing just that one](/scenarios/the-wrong-counter-on-my-lock-screen) does the job better than burying it in a group of eight.
