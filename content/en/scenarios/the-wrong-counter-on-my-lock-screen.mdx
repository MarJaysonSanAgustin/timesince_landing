Someone asked for this on the feedback board: can you pick what shows up on the lock-screen widget?

The answer was yes, with instructions, because you already can. That is worth a page of its own, because a feature nobody can find is not meaningfully different from one that does not exist.

## Where the control actually is

Not in TimeSince. In iOS.

Long-press your Lock Screen until Customise appears, tap it, choose Lock Screen, then tap the TimeSince widget. You get a list of counters and you pick one.

This is how all Lock Screen widgets work, across every app: iOS owns that editor, and an app can supply the list but cannot open it for you or choose on your behalf. Which is precisely why people look in the app's settings, find nothing, and reasonably conclude the feature is missing.

## One widget, one counter

Each widget holds a single counter. If you want two visible, add two widgets. There is no rotation and nothing that picks for you, which is deliberate: a lock screen number is only useful if you know without thinking what it refers to.

## If a counter is not in the list

Hidden and archived counters are not offered. For hidden counters that is the whole point, since something behind Face ID appearing on your lock screen would defeat the exercise. For archived ones, bring the counter back to active and it will appear.

## A bug worth knowing about

The same thread turned up a real one: the lock-screen widget could show an out-of-date time after you logged. The developer confirmed the fix shipped in the next update. If you are seeing a stale number, check your version before changing anything else.
