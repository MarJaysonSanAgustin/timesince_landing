The request on the feedback board was specific: a way to duplicate an existing counter, but with all the events cleared. The person making it had several counters in the same category, with the same colour, and was creating each one from scratch.

It shipped. Long-press a counter, or open it and use the menu, and choose Duplicate.

## Why this matters more than it sounds

A counter is five decisions: name, colour, icon, category, and any custom fields you have defined. Four of those are usually identical across a related set, and only the name genuinely changes.

Repeating four decisions to make one change is the kind of small friction that does not announce itself. You do not decide to stop tracking things. You just find that adding the next counter never quite happens, and you end up tracking nine of the twelve you meant to. It is the same friction that decides whether [a log gets made at all](/guides/log-a-habit-without-opening-the-app), one step earlier in the process.

## Set the first one up properly

Since everything afterwards inherits it, the first counter in a set is worth some care. Pick the colour you want the whole group to share, set the category, and define the custom fields once. A duplicate carries all of it.

What it does not carry is history. The new counter arrives empty, which is what you want, and starts counting when you log it for the first time.

## When duplicating is not the answer

If you are building a whole category from nothing, the Explore screen has a large library of counter ideas, and picking from it is faster than naming a dozen things yourself.

And if you have hit the three-counter limit on the free tier, duplicating will not get you past it. Pro removes the cap; a lapsed subscription archives the extras rather than deleting them, so nothing is lost either way.
